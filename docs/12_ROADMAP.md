Sprint\n```\nMonday: Plan week (1 hour)\nTuesday-Thursday: Development (8 hours/day)\nFriday: Demo & Review (2 hours)\n```\n\n### Branch Strategy\n- `main` — Production (tagged releases)\n- `dev` —
